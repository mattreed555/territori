# Capture-points rule trial (2026-09-26)

Ruleset `8x8-25-permanent-majority-single-opening-capture-points-v2`: final points are controlled territory plus opposing stones captured. The existing largest connected territory decides tied points, then a draw. The capture ledger stores stones **lost** by each color, so Teal earns `captured.red`, Apricot earns `captured.blue`. Other move, supply, ending, and repetition rules are unchanged. UI, sharing, browser result events, full and fast terminal search use the new objective. Bundled policy and style weights remain the frozen v1 territory-trained priors; the candidate ID marks their reuse, not retraining. Historical v1 datasets and their results remain distinct from v2 exports.

Exploratory local-only smoke, seed 2412, all 25 ordered pairs of five strategies, epsilon .03, intent strength/margin 1/1, two seeded color streams, max 160 plies: 25 completed, zero truncated/aborted/repetition endings, mean 81.36 plies, 445 immediate place-then-opponent-remove pairs. Aggressive Teal versus Balanced Apricot had **31** such pairs in 92 plies, final points 16–61. Thus capture points penalize the repeated sacrifice in the final score, but the existing bot still often chooses it; this trial does not establish that the loop is solved or that game balance improved. A temporary in-memory margin .5 comparison on the same 25 pairings had 457 pairs (versus 445 at margin 1), so that tuning was not shipped. The smoke event trace was deterministically replayed: 2,034 decisions and outcomes reproduced. No Jev calls or new model training. The old-rule 200-game Jev acceptance does not transfer to v2.

Focused tests cover capture-credit direction, equal territory with different captures, points tied but area unequal, terminal search respecting true points winner, and incremental leaf versus full transition parity. Final 71-test suite passed.
